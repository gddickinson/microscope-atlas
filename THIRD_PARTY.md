# Third-party material

The Microscope Atlas code and its own written content are under the MIT licence in `LICENSE`. Exceptions:

- `data/images/`: images and videos from Wikimedia Commons and from open-access papers. Each file keeps its own
  licence (public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA), recorded with its author and source in `data/images/manifest.json`
  and shown beside the image in the app.
- `data/fluorophores.json`, `data/spectra.json`: fluorophore data and spectra from FPbase (https://www.fpbase.org),
  under FPbase's terms.
- System, component and paper entries are facts with a source URL or DOI on each entry; the linked pages and papers
  belong to their publishers.
